# Acknowledgements

A huge thank you to the developers and maintainers of the **pyroomacoustics** library.

This project (`shroom`) relies heavily on `pyroomacoustics` for its core room simulation capabilities, specifically the robust and efficient implementation of the Image Source Method (ISM). Their work provides the foundation upon which our spherical harmonics and Ambisonics processing is built.

We are grateful for their contribution to the open-source audio processing community.

You can find their excellent work here: [pyroomacoustics on GitHub](https://github.com/LCAV/pyroomacoustics)
